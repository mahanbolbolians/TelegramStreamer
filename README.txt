========================================================================
               TelegramStreamer — Telegram to ADM Streamer
========================================================================

Forward any video, audio, or file from Telegram to your bot, and get
a direct high-speed download link for ADM (Advanced Download Manager)
on Android or PC with multi-threading and resume support!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO RUN ON THIS PC:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Simply double-click "start.bat".
2. Wait until you see:
   [+] Telegram Bot Connected: @GxDataCenterBot
   [+] Local Wi-Fi IP: http://192.168.100.4:8080
3. On your phone, open Telegram and send /start to @GxDataCenterBot.
4. Forward any video/file to the bot.
5. Tap/copy the link and download in ADM!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO RUN ON YOUR WINDOWS LAPTOP:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy the "TelegramStreamer" folder (or the ZIP) to your laptop.
2. Make sure Python 3.10+ is installed on your laptop (from python.org).
3. Double-click "setup.bat" (it will automatically install everything).
4. Double-click "start.bat" to run!
   (The bot will automatically detect your laptop's Wi-Fi IP).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ADM RECOMMENDED SETTINGS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- In ADM Settings -> Downloading:
  * Number of threads: Set to 8 or 16 (for maximum speed).
  * Auto-resume: Enabled.

Enjoy!
